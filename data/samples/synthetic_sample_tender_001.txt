TITLE: Renovation and technical upgrade of a municipal office building

OBJECT OF THE CONTRACT:
The contracting authority seeks a contractor for the renovation of an existing
three-storey municipal office building, including replacement of the facade
cladding, upgrade of the HVAC and ventilation system, and full renewal of the
electrical installation.

TECHNICAL SCOPE:
- Replacement of the external facade and insulation panels.
- Installation of a new HVAC system with mechanical ventilation.
- Renewal of the electrical distribution boards and wiring.
- Upgrade of the fire detection and fire safety systems.
- Connection works for water supply and sewer drainage.
- Resurfacing of the access road and adjacent infrastructure.

SITE CONSTRAINTS:
The building dates from the 1970s. An asbestos survey is referenced but the
report is not attached to this notice. Work must be phased while part of the
building remains occupied.

INFORMATION NOTE:
Detailed structural drawings and the energy performance assessment are not
included in this notice and must be requested separately.
